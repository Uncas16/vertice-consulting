Purpose

Mission, vision and values

Mission

We support financial institutions in their critical technology decisions and projects (core system selection and implementation, program management and PMO, transformation and cloud migration) with a senior team that combines financial-industry experience and technical depth, independent of vendors and committed to leaving lasting capabilities with every client.

Vision

To be Latin America's reference consultancy for banks, insurers and financial institutions transforming their technology with certainty: projects that land on time and deliver the promised value.

Our values

  1. 01

    Rigor

    We decide with data, document what we do and measure progress against criteria agreed from the start.

  2. 02

    Independence

    We don't sell software or take vendor commissions: our recommendation serves only the client's interest.

  3. 03

    Transparency

    We say what we see, even when it's uncomfortable: risks, delays and deviations are reported early.

  4. 04

    Commitment to outcomes

    We measure ourselves by what the client achieves, not by the hours we bill.

  5. 05

    Knowledge transfer

    We work side by side with the client's teams so the capability stays in-house when we leave.
